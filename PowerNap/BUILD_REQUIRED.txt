PowerNap v0.4.0-multiengine

A fresh APK must be built on a workstation with Android SDK 36 and network access to Google Maven.
This revision adds:

    androidx.health:health-services-client:1.1.0

Build Samsung flavor:

    ./gradlew :app:assembleSamsungDebug

Expected APK:

    app/build/outputs/apk/samsung/debug/app-samsung-debug.apk

Install:

    adb -s "$ANDROID_SERIAL" install -r app/build/outputs/apk/samsung/debug/app-samsung-debug.apk

Before the first nap test, open the app and press CHECK DETECTORS to verify whether
USER_ACTIVITY_ASLEEP is exposed by Health Services on the watch.
